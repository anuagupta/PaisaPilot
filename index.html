Why this doesn't auto-apply changes: reading a bank's T&C page and correctly extracting an exact reward rate is genuinely error-prone — a wrong auto-applied number could cost you real money and you'd have no reason to double-check it. So this searches the web for recent news on each of your active cards and shows you the results with links — you read them and edit My Cards yourself if something changed. Uses the same backend as Smart Buy (Settings above).

About
PaisaPilot — a private, on-device card-picker. Seeded from your own reward notes; edit anything under My Cards as terms change. Built for one person's wallet, not a financial product — always sanity-check a big purchase against your bank's current T&Cs.
